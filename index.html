01 — ABOUT

More than a developer. A problem solver.

I've always been the person who wants to figure out how things work—and, more importantly, how to make them work better.

Hello, my name is Kelvin, and I am from the state of Mississippi. My interest in technology started long before I began formally studying software development. As a student, I discovered an early aptitude for working with computers, earning the highest grade in a computer-based math course and receiving an award for my performance.

That interest followed me throughout my career. I upgraded computers, worked with hardware, configured televisions and audio equipment, worked with DJ and audiovisual systems, and eventually began building websites.

What began with WordPress eventually developed into freelance web development. I worked directly with small-business owners to understand their needs, build and customize websites, manage domains and hosting, modify CSS, and create promotional content for social media.

Eventually, I wanted to go beyond modifying existing tools. I wanted to understand how to build the tools themselves.

That led me to programming.

Today, I'm developing my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, FastAPI, SQL, APIs, databases, authentication, and full-stack application development.

I enjoy taking complicated ideas and breaking them into smaller, manageable pieces. When I encounter something I don't know, I research it, experiment, study documentation, use AI tools appropriately, and continue working until I understand the solution.
